What still needs to be put in writing?
  • Exact compensation-protection language, permitted exclusions, payment schedule, physical-exam conditions and any waiver consequences.
  • Option notice method, exercise deadline and the rulebook in force on the contract date.
  • Applicable cap exception or available cap room, service time, rights, maximum salary, permissible raises and term limits.
  • Trade eligibility and any legally available consent right. No veto, kicker or assignment protection is offered here.
  • The final contract version and required documents. No checklist entry in this preview validates a legal agreement.

Declining A1 only declines that proposal. It does not terminate a contract or create free-agent eligibility. A reoffer is not assumed.
