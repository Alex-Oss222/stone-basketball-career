## Restricted branch: your team can match

This is an independent demonstration in which Example Player is assumed to be an eligible restricted free agent and Miami has preserved its right of first refusal. It does not convert the main UFA scenario or the live Wade checkpoint into an RFA event.

For the 2003 rules, an external offer sheet needs at least three non-option seasons. B1 meets that term condition; C1 and D1 do not. Other eligibility and cap checks remain necessary. The original club's 15-day period starts on actual receipt of the executed offer sheet. A timely valid match binds the player to that club's matched principal terms; there is no second destination choice. See [1999 CBA, Article XI, Section 6](https://ipmall.law.unh.edu/sites/default/files/hosted_resources/SportsEntLaw_Institute/1999NBA_NBPA_CBA.pdf).

### What happens after an outside offer sheet

| State | What you see | Permitted next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outside offer selected, not executed | B1 details and missing signing checks | Confirm eligibility and terms; selection alone is not service |
| Executed, receipt unverified | Signed version locked; fifth panel waiting for receipt evidence | Record actual receipt; do not start a clock from the player's click |
| Received, response pending | B1 frozen; fifth panel shows sourced deadline | Wait for the original club's actual response; other offers unavailable for selection |
| Valid match recorded | Miami B1-M1 pinned fifth, exact matched terms visible | Record the binding outcome and complete remaining administration |
| Verified period ends without match | Fifth panel says “Not matched”; outside result points to B1 | Record the outside-club outcome under the applicable process |
| Deadline, notice or terms disputed | Explicit unresolved status and evidence needed | Resolve the issue before assigning a destination |

The interface does not use a fresh “Accept” button to override a binding matching outcome. A pending registration flag also does not mean the player can reopen an otherwise binding choice. The exact physical, notice and dispute conditions belong in the [contract research](../../templates/player_milestones/contract_negotiation_research.md); unresolved conditions block an unsupported final career writeback.
