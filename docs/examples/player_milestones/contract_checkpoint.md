**Scenario date: June 20, 2003.** You have a $5,000,000 player option for the next season. Your representative has supplied an illustrative signed clause requiring notice by June 25 at 5 p.m. Eastern. That date is a scenario term, not a universal option deadline.

**Decision belongs to you.** No selection has been made.

## What you are choosing

| Factor | Exercise the option | Decline the option | Still to establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Next-season salary | $5.0M base; assumed fully protected once exercised | No replacement salary secured | Full protection clause |
| Team | Club Harbor under the existing contract | No next club selected | Resulting rights and available negotiating dates |
| Market | Continue on the remaining terms | Seek eligible free-agent opportunities | Verified UFA/RFA determination |
| Role | Current coach described a rotation role | No outside role agreed | Ask current coach about lead-guard reps |
| Risk | Commit to the option year | Offers may be lower, shorter, conditional or absent | No written outside offer exists |

The upcoming season's actual cap is not yet published at this example date. A future published cap must not be pulled backward to manufacture a precise dollar valuation. The [market profile baseline](../../player_market_profile.md) keeps an unavailable valuation visible when required evidence is missing.

## Representative's open questions

1. Confirm the exact notice delivery method and receipt evidence.
2. Determine the rights that follow an option decline. “Free to decline” does not establish “unrestricted.”
3. Confirm whether a lawful extension is available and whether Harbor is making one.

**Possible instruction:** “Confirm my free-agent status if I decline and ask Harbor about extension terms. I have not exercised or declined the option.”

**Next checkpoint:** answers before the sourced deadline. If no answer arrives, the unresolved record still needs a genuine player decision and applicable notice/default review. The interface does not choose automatically.

### How the team-option version changes

If Harbor owns the same option, the main action becomes **Ask for the club's decision and role explanation**. The exercise/decline controls disappear. The player may express a preference; Harbor owns that decision. Once its valid notice is recorded, the page shows the consequence and any new player choices.
