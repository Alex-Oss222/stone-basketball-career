Absolutely — here’s a broad **2004 NBA free-agency list**, covering the offseason between the **2003–04 and 2004–05 seasons**.

The first three sections contain **141 veteran names**, including players who changed teams, re-signed, remained unsigned, or signed short-lived preseason contracts. I’ve also included additional signings from overseas, the undrafted market, and players released during the offseason.

**How to read it:** Teams in parentheses are the players’ last **2003–04 NBA teams**. **S&T** means *sign-and-trade*. A **†** identifies a player selected by the Charlotte Bobcats in the expansion draft before becoming a free agent; their earlier team is shown for clarity.
