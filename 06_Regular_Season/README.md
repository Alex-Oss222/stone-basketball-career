# Regular Season

The regular season uses month folders and exactly four week buckets per month.

Week 1 is days 1 to 7. Week 2 is 8 to 14. Week 3 is 15 to 21. Week 4 is day 22 through the end of the month.

Only the requested active-season span is precreated: October Weeks 3-4, November through March Weeks 1-4, and April Weeks 1-2.

A week note with `status: not_started` is not a game and not evidence of an event. Actual games should be recorded in the week only after they are scheduled or played.
