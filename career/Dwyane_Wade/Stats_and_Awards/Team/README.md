## Coverage

Each season's register is a team-control record, not a finalized active roster: it labels contracts, options, rights held without a contract and departures. Only closed Miami games enter these tables, and a period's page lists only the players Miami held under contract in it; a not-started page lists the register's players under contract and names the rights it holds.

Preserve departures on earlier pages and on the season line for their Miami games. Add arrivals from their first applicable period. A team record counts each game once; summing player G would overcount games. Keep playoffs separate: each season's playoff games are on its playoff page.

## Updating

Use the [shared statistics rules](../README.md) for denominators, DNPs and weighted rollups. Reconcile team scoring with the player lines. Where the kernel records unassigned team turnovers, team TOV includes those in addition to individual TOV; never attribute them to a player to force a match. Averages with no appearances and percentages with no attempts remain N/A.
