<summary>Voting procedure and eligibility</summary>

Electorates, ballots and announcement dates are researched for the season (`library/2004/league/nba_2004_05_season_awards.json`); every ballot is filed by the rule in `runtime/season_awards.py`.

| Award | Announced | Voters | Ballot |

| Most Improved Player | April 28, 2005 | 123 media | 5-3-1 |
| Defensive Player of the Year | May 2, 2005 | 125 media | 5-3-1 |
| Sixth Man of the Year | May 3, 2005 | 125 media | 5-3-1 |
| Rookie of the Year | May 4, 2005 | 126 media | 5-3-1 |
| All-Rookie Teams | May 5, 2005 | 30 head coaches, not for their own players | 2-1 |
| Most Valuable Player | May 8, 2005 | 127 media | 10-7-5-3-1 |
| Coach of the Year | May 10, 2005 | 126 media | 5-3-1 |
| All-Defensive Teams | May 12, 2005 | 30 head coaches, not for their own players | 2-1 |
| All-NBA Teams | May 18, 2005 | 124 media | 5-3-1 |
| Finals MVP | the night the Finals are clinched | 10 media panel | 1 |

Every voter in the real electorate files a ballot from closed simulated results; voters differ only in how much they weigh the award's two published criteria, so no vote is chance and nothing is re-rolled. All-NBA and All-Defensive teams keep the era's positional slots (two guards, two forwards, one center); All-Rookie teams take five players regardless of position, and a coach never votes for his own players. Every ballot or the complete tally stays in the owning award record; this page shows three finishers.

</details>
