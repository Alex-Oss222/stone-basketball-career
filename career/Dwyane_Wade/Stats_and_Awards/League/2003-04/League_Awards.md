# 2003-04 NBA Awards Voting

**League:** NBA  
**Status:** Voting not yet conducted  
**Display rule:** Publish the top three vote-getters for each individual award. Mark the No. 1 row **WINNER**.

## Voting model

The branch uses era-shaped media voting rather than selecting winners directly from one statistic.

- **Most Valuable Player:** 123 media ballots, five ranked choices, 10-7-5-3-1 points. The 2003-04 historical award had 123 possible first-place votes and was media-voted.
- **Rookie of the Year:** 118 media ballots, three ranked choices, 5-3-1 points. The NBA's 2003-04 announcement explicitly used this system.
- **Defensive Player of the Year, Sixth Man of the Year, Most Improved Player:** three ranked choices, 5-3-1 points. Unless a period-specific electorate is verified before the vote closes, the simulation uses 118 ballots for continuity.
- Ballots are generated from branch evidence only: availability, role, production, team results and award-specific eligibility. Real 2003-04 winners and votes are not imported.
