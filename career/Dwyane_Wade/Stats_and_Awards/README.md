# Dwyane Wade | Stats & Awards

[2003-04](2003-04/README.md)

This is the chronological statistical and honors record for the simulated career.

## Structure

```text
Stats_and_Awards/
  2003-04/
    README.md
    10_October/
      README.md
      Week_3/README.md
      Week_4/README.md
    ...
```

Week pages own game logs and weekly aggregates. Month pages roll up completed weeks. Year pages roll up months and keep regular season and playoffs separate.

## Statistical convention

The basic line follows standard NBA box-score presentation: games, starts when available, minutes, points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers and shooting percentages. Detail tables retain makes and attempts, offensive and defensive rebounds, and personal fouls when the game engine supplies them.

Actual numbers come only from closed simulation game results. Real-world Dwyane Wade statistics are never imported as branch results.

## Awards convention

NBA weekly award periods can cross this project's fixed calendar buckets. Preserve the official award dates and file a weekly honor on the week page containing the award period's end date. Monthly honors live on the month page; season honors live on the year page.
