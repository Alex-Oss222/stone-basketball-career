## Statistics conventions

G and GS are counts. MPG, PPG, RPG, APG, SPG, BPG and TOV/G are per appearance. Shooting tables show total makes/attempts and percentages; GS stays N/A when starts are unavailable. DNPs do not increase G. Scheduled games are not results.

Combine raw totals first. Recompute averages with total appearances and percentages with total attempts; never average weekly percentages or add a season subtotal to its component weeks. Zero attempts produce N/A, not 0%. In a genuinely empty period, count totals are zero and rates remain N/A. An incomplete feed is missing coverage, not proof of zero production.

eFG% = (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) / FGA. Estimated TS% = PTS / [2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA)]. Display percentages to one decimal and per-game figures to one decimal, while retaining full precision in the underlying game totals. Team wins/losses count games, not player appearances. Regular season and playoffs stay separate.
