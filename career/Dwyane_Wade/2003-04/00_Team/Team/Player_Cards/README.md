Snapshot date: June 26, 2003.

Every player in the team-control register has a card using [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) as the canonical format.

The template's section order is enforced by repository validation: scouting report, player grades, changes/coaching notes, sources/uncertainty, regular-season statistics, playoff statistics, then awards and awards last.

Headshots and license/credit metadata come from the league source files when available. Subjective grades remain `Not assessed` until dated Miami staff evidence supports them.

Fourteen veteran cards now include recorded 2002-03 production and separate 20–80 statistical estimates. A grade of 50 is the median of the reference cohort, not an overall player rating. Read the [method](../../../../../../docs/statistical_ratings.md) for sample adjustment and engine mapping. Mourning has no eligible record; the two incoming rookies have not been regraded. Regular-season history, playoff history and awards remain the final three sections.
