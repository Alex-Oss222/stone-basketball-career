The cap sheet uses the standard multi-year NBA presentation: player commitments across seasons, options and cap holds separated from active contracts, then a team summary.

The 2003-era CBA does not have modern first- and second-apron accounting, so those rows are not used. The historical 2003-04 cap was $43,840,000, but it was not announced until July 15. Free-agent holds, incomplete-roster charges, pending options and unused exceptions must be reconciled before Miami has certified usable cap room.
