# Miami Heat six-season cap tracker

**Snapshot:** June 26, 2003  
**Horizon:** 2003-04 through 2008-09  
**Amounts:** whole US dollars

This tracker uses real historical NBA salary-cap limits for continuity. Future cap limits are reference-only until the simulation reaches the season and the league's publication point.
