## Free-agent holds still to reconcile

Alonzo Mourning · Malik Allen · Travis Best · LaPhonso Ellis · Eddie House · Mike James · Ken Johnson · Sean Marks · Vladimir Stepania

Under the 1999 CBA, unrenounced free agents can remain in team salary. A team with fewer than 11 counted players can also carry incomplete-roster charges. Until those items and Miami's unused exceptions are reconciled, the difference between commitments and the cap is not spendable cap room.
